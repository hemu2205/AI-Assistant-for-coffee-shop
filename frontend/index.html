receipt_long Refund & Cancellation Policy

Prepared orders cannot be cancelled. If your order is incorrect or damaged, we offer an immediate counter replacement or a 100% refund credited within 24 hours.

eco BYO Cup & Sustainability Discount

Bring your own clean reusable mug and enjoy an instant ₹15 discount on any espresso or cold beverage! All our packaging is 100% biodegradable.

wifi Wi-Fi, Seating & Power Outlets

Free high-speed Wi-Fi (BrewBuddy_Guest, password: brewbuddycoffee). Dedicated work tables with power outlets. Peak-hour seating limit: 2 hours.

pets Pet & Dog Friendly Seating
