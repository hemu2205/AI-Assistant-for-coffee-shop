Frequently Asked Questions & Policies:

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: If your drink or food is incorrect or defective, we offer an immediate replacement or full refund credited within 24 hours. Prepared orders cannot be cancelled.

Q: Do you offer discounts for bringing my own cup?
A: Yes! Bring your reusable mug and get ₹15 off any coffee or tea beverage.

Q: Are pets allowed in the coffee shop?
A: Yes! Pets and dogs are allowed in our outdoor patio seating area with free water bowls.

Q: What is your Wi-Fi policy and password?
A: High-speed Wi-Fi (BrewBuddy_Guest) is free for all customers. Password: brewbuddycoffee. Peak hour seating limit is 2 hours.

Q: How do bulk or catering orders work?
A: For orders over 10 items, please pre-order 2 hours in advance. Orders over ₹2000 receive a 10% discount.

Q: What dairy-free milk alternatives do you offer?
A: We offer Oat Milk (+₹30) and Almond Milk (+₹30) for any coffee or tea beverage.

Q: Which drinks are lowest in caffeine?
A: Green Tea (low), Vanilla Frappé (low), or Decaf Espresso. Snacks are caffeine-free.

Q: Do you have vegan food options?
A: Yes! Our Veg Sandwich on sourdough is 100% vegan and dairy-free.

Q: Are there keto-friendly beverages?
A: Yes, Espresso, Americano, Iced Americano, and Cold Brew without milk/sugar are keto-friendly.

Q: How is tax calculated?
A: A 5% GST is applied to the order subtotal.
